About

Zivo Studio

Zivo Studio is an independent game studio. We're a small team that designs, builds, and ships original games ourselves — from the first sketch of an idea to the code that runs it.

Our first release, Shadow Wings, grew out of a simple question: how much tension can you get from one tap and one bird? The rest of the game — the checkpoints, the power-ups, the pacing — was built around answering that.

We're not a big studio with a big catalog. We'd rather make a handful of games that feel deliberate than a long list of ones that don't. Shadow Wings is the first; more are on the way.

Guide

How to Play Shadow Wings

  1. 1 Tap to fly. One tap lifts the bird; letting go brings it back down. That's the whole control scheme — the challenge is in the timing.
  2. 2 Read the sky. Obstacles come at a steady pace and get less forgiving the further you go. Watch what's ahead, not just what's in front of you.
  3. 3 Hit the checkpoints. Clearing a checkpoint locks in your progress, so a mistake later doesn't send you all the way back to the start.
  4. 4 Grab power-ups when you see them. They're placed to reward tight flying, not to bail you out of a bad run.
  5. 5 Expect to restart. Runs are short on purpose. Losing a run costs you seconds, not minutes, so the next attempt starts fast.

FAQ

About Shadow Wings

What is Shadow Wings? Shadow Wings is an original one-tap flying game by Zivo Studio. The goal is to guide the bird through obstacles, reach checkpoints, and finish the run without crashing.

How do the controls work? The game is designed around a simple touch control: tap to lift the bird and use timing to control its movement through the course.

What makes each run different? Checkpoints divide the run into stages, while power-ups reward accurate flying. The challenge is built around short attempts and improving your timing.

Where can I play? Shadow Wings is designed for mobile-friendly play through the supported web game experience. Use the Play Now button on this site to launch the game.
